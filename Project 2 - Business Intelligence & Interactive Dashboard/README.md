# Project 2 - Business Intelligence & Interactive Dashboard

## Overview

This project was completed as part of the **AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship — Week 2**.

The objective was to develop an executive Business Intelligence dashboard using **Microsoft Power BI** to help management monitor sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, and regional performance.

The project transforms Superstore sales data into an interactive dashboard designed to support data-driven business decision-making.

## Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:

1. What is the overall sales performance of the company?
2. Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
3. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
4. Which product categories perform best?
5. Which products are the most profitable?
6. What trends can be observed over time?
7. What actions should management take to improve business performance?

## Tools Used

- Total Sales

## Key Insights

- Sales increased from approximately **$470.53K in 2015 to $733.22K in 2017**, indicating strong overall growth.
- The **West region** generated the highest sales at approximately **$725.46K**.
- The **Consumer segment** contributed **50.56% of total sales**, making it the largest revenue-generating segment.
- **Technology** was the highest-performing product category, generating approximately **$836K in sales**.
- Several states generated substantial sales while reporting negative profit, demonstrating the importance of monitoring profitability alongside revenue.

## Business Risks

- Profitability pressure in loss-making markets.
- Uneven regional performance.
- Dependence on the Consumer customer segment.

## Business Opportunities

- Expand high-performing Technology products.
- Improve profitability in underperforming markets.
- Grow Corporate and Home Office customer segments.

## Recommendations

1. Prioritize profitability alongside revenue when evaluating business performance.
2. Increase investment in high-performing Technology products.
3. Develop targeted strategies for weaker regions.
4. Investigate pricing, discounts, shipping costs, and product mix in loss-making states.
5. Diversify customer revenue by strengthening Corporate and Home Office segments.
